# Turkish glossary

The same word is used on every platform. A change here needs the owner's sign-off.

Register: buttons are bare imperatives (Kaydet, Tara). Instructions and hints use the polite siz-form (Ön yüzleri tarayın). Messages say what happened and what to do. Confirmations ask with the passive -sın mı? form (Sayfa silinsin mi?, Tarama durdurulsun mu?). Buttons, menu items and titles of actions are in title case, as in Apple's Turkish (Görüntüyü Kaydet, Sayfayı Sil); sentences, hints and messages are in sentence case. A dotted İ and a dotless ı keep their case in capitals (kağıt, KAĞIT; ilk, İLK). Nouns stay singular after numbers (3 sayfa). A placeholder never takes a suffix.

Platform chrome may differ: Cancel is Vazgeç on the iPhone and the Mac, İptal on Windows.
