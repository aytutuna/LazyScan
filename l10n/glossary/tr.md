# Turkish glossary

The same word is used on every platform unless the Windows section says otherwise. A change here needs the owner's sign-off.

Register: buttons are bare imperatives (Kaydet, Tara). Instructions and hints use the polite siz-form (Ön yüzleri tarayın). Messages say what happened and what to do. Confirmations ask with the passive -sın mı? form (Sayfa silinsin mi?, Tarama durdurulsun mu?). Buttons and menu items are in title case on the iPhone and the Mac, as in Apple's Turkish (Görüntüyü Kaydet), and in sentence case on Windows (Farklı kaydet); a shared button that differs gets its Windows form in the `win` section. Sentences, hints and messages are in sentence case everywhere. Capitals keep the dot: i becomes İ and ı becomes I (ilk, İLK; kağıt, KAĞIT). Nouns stay singular after numbers (3 sayfa). A placeholder never takes a suffix. The tables give the sentence-case form; the casing rule above decides how it is written in a button. A placeholder that holds a name (scanner, printer, phone, computer, network, file, folder) is rebuilt around so it stands alone: after a colon, before a separate word such as adlı, ile or için, or inside “…” ({0} adlı tarayıcıya bağlanılamadı; Bağlanılamadı: {0}; {0} ile eşleştirildi). The stand-ins for a missing name (the scanner, a phone, this network) are written so they read in that same slot. A suffix on a name or an abbreviation takes an apostrophe, the typographic ’ as Apple writes it (Finder’da, Ayarlar’ı, Word’e), on Windows too, and follows how the letters are read: PDF’ye, PDF’yi, PDF’nin, PDF’ler; QR kodu. Quotation marks are “…”. The ellipsis is the single character … (Yazdır…). The per cent sign goes before the number (%100) and is spoken as yüzde ({0} percent is yüzde {0}). Decimals take a comma (2,5°) and thousands a dot (65.535). A number and its unit are separated by a space (300 dpi, 5 MB, 210 × 297 mm). Wi-Fi stays Wi-Fi, as Apple and Microsoft write it.

Platform chrome may differ: Cancel is Vazgeç on the iPhone and the Mac, İptal on Windows; Rename is Adını Değiştir on the iPhone and the Mac, Yeniden adlandır on Windows (see Windows).
