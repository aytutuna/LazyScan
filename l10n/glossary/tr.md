# Turkish glossary

The same word is used on every platform. A change here needs the owner's sign-off.

Register: buttons are bare imperatives (Kaydet, Tara). Instructions and hints use the polite siz-form (Ön yüzleri tarayın). Messages say what happened and what to do. Confirmations ask with the passive -sın mı? form (Sayfa silinsin mi?, Tarama durdurulsun mu?). Buttons and menu items are in title case on the iPhone and the Mac, as in Apple's Turkish (Görüntüyü Kaydet), and in sentence case on Windows (Farklı kaydet); a shared button that differs gets its Windows form in the `win` section. Sentences, hints and messages are in sentence case everywhere. Capitals keep the dot: i becomes İ and ı becomes I (ilk, İLK; kağıt, KAĞIT). Nouns stay singular after numbers (3 sayfa). A placeholder never takes a suffix.

Platform chrome may differ: Cancel is Vazgeç on the iPhone and the Mac, İptal on Windows.
