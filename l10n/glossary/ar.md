# Arabic glossary

The same word is used on every platform. A change here needs the owner's sign-off.

Register: Modern Standard Arabic, right to left. Buttons, menu items and tabs are verbal nouns, as in Apple's Arabic (حفظ، إلغاء، مشاركة، طباعة), never gendered imperatives. Hints and instructions are rebuilt as noun phrases or neutral statements so no gender shows. Messages say what happened and what to do. Confirmations are short questions ending in the Arabic question mark ؟ (حذف الصفحة؟). Arabic has no letter case, so titles, buttons and sentences follow one rule on every platform; Latin names keep their own spelling (Lazy Scan, PDF, TWAIN). A quoted name or label goes in Apple's straight double quotes "...". The comma is ، and the question mark is ؟; the ellipsis is the single character … directly after the word (طباعة…). A number and a unit are written with a space and the Latin unit ({0} dpi, {0} MB), except millimetres, which take CLDR's Arabic مم ({0} مم); digits arrive only through placeholders and are never typed. A placeholder that holds a name (printer, scanner, phone, file, network, folder) takes no prefix, article, suffix or gender agreement: put the noun before it and keep the name as it is (الماسح {0}, الشبكة {0}), and never join ب, ل, و or ف to it. Add no direction marks; the app isolates Latin text itself. Counts use all six plural categories, each a real form (last table below).

Platform chrome may differ: a few Windows labels use Microsoft's wording (see Windows).

## Plural forms of the commonest nouns

| Category | page | scan | photo |
|---|---|---|---|
| zero | لا صفحات | لا نسخ ضوئية | لا صور |
| one | صفحة واحدة | نسخة ضوئية واحدة | صورة واحدة |
| two | صفحتان | نسختان ضوئيتان | صورتان |
| few | {0} صفحات | {0} نسخ ضوئية | {0} صور |
| many | {0} صفحة | {0} نسخة ضوئية | {0} صورة |
| other | {0} صفحة | {0} نسخة ضوئية | {0} صورة |

The few form (3 to 10) takes the plural noun; many (11 to 99) and other take the singular noun.
