# German glossary

The same word is used on every platform. A change here needs the owner's sign-off.

Register: du, as in Apple's German prompts („%@“ möchte auf deine Kamera zugreifen; Erlaube „%@“ in den Systemeinstellungen, auf die Kamera zuzugreifen), with du, dich and dein in lower case, never Sie. Buttons are bare infinitives (Sichern, Scannen, Drucken). Hints and instructions use the du imperative (Lege die Seite auf das Vorlagenglas; Tippe auf „Scannen“; Klicke auf „Sichern“; Wähle einen Ordner). Messages say what happened and what to do in a short sentence, with no Bitte and no exclamation mark (Versuche es erneut.). Confirmations ask a short question with the object (Seite löschen?, Scan abbrechen?). Casing follows Apple on every platform: buttons, menu items, tabs and titles are sentence case, so only the first word, nouns and names take a capital (Als PDF sichern, Dokumente scannen, Bild sichern); there is no title case. Whole sentences end with a full stop. Quotation marks are „…“, with ‚…‘ inside them, also around names Apple quotes („Dateien“, „Fotos“); never "…" or »…«. The ellipsis is the single character … after a no-break space, as Apple writes it (Drucken …, Scannen …), on Windows too. A number and its unit or % are separated by a no-break space ({0} dpi, {0} MB, {0} %). Counts take one and other ({0} Seite, {0} Seiten). A placeholder that holds a name (printer, scanner, phone, file, network, folder) takes no article, ending or case: rebuild the sentence so the name stands alone, after a colon or inside „…“ (Drucker nicht erreichbar: {0}; „{0}“ wurde getrennt.). PDF is neuter (das PDF, ein durchsuchbares PDF). Compounds make buttons long, so a button is one word, or two where Apple uses two. Text the app shows in capitals, such as the kind titles in the camera row, is written in normal case and stays one short word; the system turns ß into SS (SCHLIESSEN), never ẞ. Wi-Fi is WLAN, as Apple and Microsoft write it.

Platform chrome may differ: Save is Sichern on the iPhone and the Mac, Speichern on Windows; Allow is Erlauben on the iPhone and the Mac, Zulassen on Windows (see Windows).

## Windows

Windows-only overrides for the win section. A key shared with the iPhone or the Mac keeps the Apple form in the shared table, and its Windows form goes here. Only buttons, labels, tabs and titles get one; sentences and counted text keep the Apple word on every platform. Everything not listed reads the same on Windows (Abbrechen, Passwort, Schwarzweiß, Dokumenteinzug, Fehlerbehebung, Im Ordner zeigen).

| Save as (format picker) | Sichern als | Speichern als |
| Save to | Sichern in | Speichern in |
| Saved (tab) | Gesichert | Gespeichert |
